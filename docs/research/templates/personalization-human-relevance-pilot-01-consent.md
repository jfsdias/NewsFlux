# Draft consent text — Personalization Human Relevance Pilot 01

**Status:** draft for owner/privacy review. Not approved for recruitment or data collection.

## About this study

We are preparing a study about how two arrangements of the same set of public news articles feel to read. One arrangement uses deterministic editorial ordering; the other applies the existing NewsFlux Personalization V1 ordering to a temporary study-only preference profile. The study is intended to understand perceived relevance, variety, freshness, repetition, organization, and preference. It does not guarantee that either arrangement is better.

## What you would do

If this study is later approved, you may choose broad topic interests and, if you agree, identify public sample articles you would personally save/star. You would then view two versions of the same Home composition under neutral labels and answer optional ratings. You may choose “cannot judge,” skip questions, or stop at any time.

The temporary study profile is created only from your explicit study choices. It is not connected to your NewsFlux or Miniflux account and is not your real reading or starred history. The study will not ask for passwords, account access, or private feeds.

## Information collected

The proposed study record contains a random study code, optional broad topic selections, study-only public sample-item choices, the ratings you choose to provide, and the version/order assignment needed to audit the comparison. It will not intentionally collect your name, email, NewsFlux username, Miniflux credentials, private reading history, or account data. Do not put personal or identifying details in an optional comment.

## Use, storage, and deletion

Responses would be used only for the approved NewsFlux research question. They would be stored on an owner-approved, access-restricted host, separately from the version-assignment key. No external AI, advertising analytics, or NewsFlux production telemetry would be used.
