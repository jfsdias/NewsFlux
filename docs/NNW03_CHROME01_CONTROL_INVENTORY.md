## 1. Classification Taxonomy

Every interactive control across the 12 NewsFlux surfaces is assigned one or more of the seven canonical classifications:

- **`GLOBAL_PERSISTENT`**: App-level navigation or shell controls that remain available across streams within their window class.
- **`CONTEXTUAL_PERSISTENT`**: Surface-specific controls that persist while viewing a given stream or reader surface.
- **`CONTEXTUAL_TRANSIENT`**: Controls that appear only when a contextual condition, mode, or capability is active (e.g., `selectionMode`, `personalizationApplied`, `hasHeadings`, collapsed desktop sidebar).
- **`DUPLICATE`**: Controls whose action is also reachable via another surface or menu (e.g., `SidebarTrigger` legacy fallback vs `CompactNavigation`/`MediumNavigation` `Sources` trigger; `Mark all as read` in `FooterPanel` vs `Sidebar` context menu on feed/category).
- **`MUST_REMAIN_VISIBLE`**: High-frequency primary triage or navigation actions that must remain top-level one-tap controls.
- **`MENU_ELIGIBLE`**: Secondary or lower-frequency controls that are candidates for overflow/popover consolidation under `CHROME-01`.
- **`DO_NOT_MOVE`**: Controls bound by frozen layout/ownership contracts (`FOOTERPANEL_OWNERSHIP=Content`, reader top bar, window-class navigation rails).

## 3. Genuine Duplications & Contextual Findings

1. **`All`, `Today`, `Starred` in `#mobile-sidebar-drawer` vs `CompactNavigation` / `MediumNavigation` (`<=992px`):**
   - On `COMPACT` (`<=600px`) and `MEDIUM` (`601px–992px`), `Home`, `All`, `Today`, and `Starred` are already persistent top-level destinations on the bottom bar / medium rail. When the user taps `Sources` (`IconFolder`) to open `#mobile-sidebar-drawer`, `SidebarMenuItems` renders `All`, `Today`, `Starred`, and `History` at the top of the drawer above the source list.
   - However, `History` (`/history`) is **only** reachable via `SidebarMenuItems` inside the drawer on `<=992px`, and existing integration harnesses (`nav-visual-pr1-compact-nav`, `nnw-02c-sidebar-keyboard`) assert `SidebarMenuItems` presence. Hiding `All`/`Today`/`Starred` inside the mobile drawer while keeping `History` is therefore classified as a `CHROME-01` product decision (`HOLD_PRODUCT_DECISION`).
2. **`FooterPanel` vs `SearchAndSortBar` Split Action Chrome:**
   - Every non-Home stream (`All`, `Today`, `Starred`, `feed`, `category`, `history`) renders two chrome bars inside `.entry-col`:
     - Top `SearchAndSortBar`: `[Select] [Search] [DateFilter?] [Sort]`
     - Bottom `FooterPanel`: `[Mark All Read] [Mark Older Read?] [Status Filter: (Starred |) Unread | All] [Refresh]`
   - On `COMPACT` (`<=600px`), `FooterPanel` sits immediately above `CompactNavigation`, creating two stacked bottom bars (`FooterPanel` + `CompactNavigation`).
   - Moving or merging `FooterPanel` controls into `SearchAndSortBar` violates `FOOTERPANEL_OWNERSHIP=Content` (`docs/NEWSFLUX_NAV_VISUAL_01.md`) and `input-parity-touch-target-01` unless explicitly approved under `CHROME-01`.

## 4. `CHROME-01` Product Decision Options & Recommendation (`HOLD_PRODUCT_DECISION`)

- **Option C1-A (Status Quo + Accessibility/Touch Hardening — Implemented in `argon/nnw-03-contextual-chrome`):**
  - Keep `SearchAndSortBar` at top and `FooterPanel` at bottom of `.entry-col` (`FOOTERPANEL_OWNERSHIP=Content`, `FOOTERPANEL_PORTAL=PROHIBITED`), with explicit `aria-label`/`aria-hidden` on `FooterPanel` status filter radiogroup and `>=44×44px` coarse-pointer targets across `Home` and `SearchModal`.
- **Option C1-B (Contextual `FooterPanel` Pruning on `/today` and Drawer Stream Deduplication):**
  - Hide `Mark older as read` (`1d/7d/30d`) on `/today` (where `1d` is an empty intersection) and hide `All`/`Today`/`Starred` from `#mobile-sidebar-drawer` on `<=992px` (leaving `History` + `Sources`), while keeping `FooterPanel` in `.entry-col`.
- **Option C1-C (Unified Top Stream Toolbar on `COMPACT` / `MEDIUM`):**
  - On `<=992px`, promote `Status Filter` (`Unread | All`) into the stream header sub-row and move `Mark older as read` + `Refresh` into a stream header overflow menu so `CompactNavigation` is the sole bottom bar on phones.
- **Recommendation:**
  - **Ship Option C1-A immediately** (implemented in `argon/nnw-03-contextual-chrome`) and **recommend Option C1-B** as the next low-risk `CHROME-01` product slice once PM approves updating the `bulk03b` `/today` harness expectation. Hold Option C1-C as `HOLD_PRODUCT_DECISION` until multi-pane (`NNW-05`) and mobile single-bottom-bar (`CHROME-01`) layout sign-off.
