# Contract freeze protocol

This stage makes no Miniflux or article-content request, processes no article body, changes no corpus and opens no human-label file. Git operations are excluded from research-content network counters. The frozen fresh corpus and D2E aggregate inputs are hash-verified; the corpus is counted without parsing article values. A local guard rejects the named human-label and joined-label paths.

The machine-readable contract pins the base, corpus and D2E hashes and encodes identity, source, temporal, normalization, privacy, security, redirect, coverage and future artifact rules. It is generated twice without runtime timestamps. An independent validator checks the contract, including the 500 denominator, 0.80 boundary, Miniflux priority, publisher HOLD and zero current content accesses.

The contract and private manifest are written via temporary files, fsync and atomic rename; both are hashed, made read-only, rehashed and independently reloaded. Inputs are rehashed after the freeze. No real article value enters the contract or tracked files.
