# D2F10 preregistered outcome analysis

Count frozen baseline and expansion decisions globally. Discovery V2 requires at least one SAME_EVENT. The historical calibration heuristic requires twelve SAME_EVENT, three positive historical D2F1 components, and a largest positive component share at most one half. It is a governance heuristic, not a statistical power or production criterion.

Do not calculate component-level label strata when the count condition fails. If it passes, reconstruct the original D2F1 article-sharing components and verify D2F2 assignments before projecting both articles of each expansion pair. Missing or cross-component articles make the historical heuristic not evaluable, without redefining components. Population prevalence remains unknown. This analysis does not authorize a benchmark. Only aggregate results enter tracked files.
