# Dense-window precision-first validation — design only

## Population and candidate rule

After the final D2G contract is merged and the rule and review plan are frozen, capture all eligible visible Miniflux entries from a new contiguous 48-hour publication window. Pair only across feeds at gaps up to 24 hours. The future window must begin after the merge, be temporally disjoint from the previous corpus and all 51 reviewed pairs, and exclude backfilled pre-window publication times. No systematic article thinning. A prespecified volume or safe-storage ceiling must be approved before capture; exceeding it stops for PM rather than changing the sample.

Proposed precision-first merge candidate: require an exact, unambiguous normalized event identifier and agreement on actor, predicate and object, within the frozen time window, plus no contradictory date, location, version, score or role evidence. Missing or conflicting evidence means abstain. Examples of identifiers include a legal case plus court/decision type; software CVE or repository plus release/version; sports teams plus fixture/competition/date; consumer product model plus launch/update; travel route plus airport/airline and specific event; or a general actor–predicate–object–time/location tuple. Extractors, normalization, conflict rules and absolute match requirements need executable preregistration and synthetic tests before capture. No percentile, residual quantile or top-K-only merge rule is permitted. The D2G0 patterns are post-hoc hypotheses, not thresholds.

Qwen title similarity is RETRIEVAL_ONLY; shared-feed graph support is RETRIEVAL_PRIORITIZATION_ONLY, because topic popularity is not proof of event identity. Neither is sufficient for a merge. No model download or execution is authorized now.

## Sampling and labels

The primary sampling population is the complete, pre-label candidate-graph component partition. Review every predicted merge when feasible; otherwise apply the BRF V1 component probability sample with known inclusion probabilities and review all predicted merges inside selected components. Never present a top-score review as representative. A separate preregistered probability control sample of non-merges covers false splits, boundary cases and hard negatives; it does not relax the primary merge-safety gate. Freeze sample sizes, sampling seeds, inclusion probabilities and the independent-unit accounting before capture.

Two reviewers independently assign SAME_EVENT, RELATED_TOPIC, DIFFERENT_EVENT or UNCERTAIN from title, source and publication time, without seeing each other's decisions. A third adjudicator resolves disagreement. RELATED_TOPIC is merge-negative; UNCERTAIN is not confirmed safe. Report raw agreement, Cohen's kappa when defined, per-reviewer UNCERTAIN and RELATED_TOPIC rates, and adjudication rate. Proposed minimum for primary validity: raw agreement at least 0.85 and kappa at least 0.60; undefined kappa or an unmet gate yields INCONCLUSIVE pending PM disposition, not automatic PASS. These proposed numbers require PM approval before validation.

For a separately seeded, probability-selected subset, independent reviewers repeat classification using frozen stored Miniflux content after title-only decisions are locked; report title-only-to-context flip rate. No publisher fetch. The context arm is not a license to relabel the primary sample opportunistically; its estimand, randomization, retention and adjudication must be frozen beforehand.

## Outcome, stopping and cluster safety

Use the component-level finite-population bound and worst-case edge conversion in BRF V1. Proposed fixed maximum: 200 sampled components, subject to PM approval and a separate cap on total edges reviewed. Determine the actual sample size before capture; perform one final statistical look only. Capture and review a complete preselected unit or classify the primary analysis INCONCLUSIVE. Do not stop because enough positives were found.

PASS requires the verified one-sided upper risk bound <=0.05, the reviewer-quality gate and zero observed false family merges among completely reviewed sampled components. FAIL requires an independently verified lower bound above 0.05 or an adjudicated catastrophic false family merge; the catastrophic rule and any multiple-decision error allocation must be approved before capture. Otherwise, including an inadequately tight bound or budget exhaustion, INCONCLUSIVE. Review all sampled edges for component contamination; report false-family-merge count and largest wrong-event component size. A pairwise pass alone cannot override the cluster guard.

## Red-team barriers

Old labels are hypothesis-only and barred from validation inputs. All candidate rules, transformations, source and time boundaries, sampling code, seed policy, inclusion probabilities, interval, one-look budget and adjudication must be frozen before the future window; a post-capture change invalidates that validation and requires a new independent window. Exhaustive component membership and complete within-component review prevent invisible cherry-picking. Unknown inclusion probabilities, oversized clusters, unverified interval coverage, insufficient reviewer agreement or missing adjudication force INCONCLUSIVE/STOP. UNCERTAIN is unsafe/unconfirmed in the primary outcome. Large syndicated stories remain whole components; worst-case edge-weight conversion penalizes their potential dominance. Cluster sampling addresses edge dependence in the frozen finite population, while any extrapolation to other windows needs separate assumptions and authorization. Old-corpus fallback, retrospective backfill and model tuning on reviewed future labels are forbidden.

The D2F content-aware benchmark is REUSE_WITH_REDESIGN: retain D2E3 privacy/provenance, normalization, hard-negative taxonomy and paired-comparison ideas, but replace its readiness rule and validation sampling. BLIND remains sealed; a future opening requires a separate immutable protocol, approved evidence and explicit authorization.
