# Targeted DEV capacity protocol

The catalog is fixed and hash-verified before old-DEV diagnostics. S0–S3 and T0–T3 are predeclared; N0 and residual controls follow the strong and text lanes. No labels guide candidate capacity. Existing DEV and pre-label BLIND article endpoints are used solely for exclusion. An access guard blocks standard file-opening and pathlib read methods for the sealed BLIND human-label file.

A raw lane shortfall proves infeasibility for any exact quota requiring more rows. When raw counts suffice, an optimal article-matching upper bound may prove a strong-lane shortfall. Such necessary-condition proofs do not establish the result of independently testing later diversity constraints. They establish only infeasibility of the tested quota contracts, not of all future retrieval strategies.

A separate maximum-total diagnostic uses an ephemeral MILP witness satisfying article disjointness and source/category caps at the vertex-count upper bound. Its aggregate lane composition is one maximum-total witness; it is not the independent maximum achievable for each lane. Witness identities are not persisted. An unresolved bounded solve is reported as unknown. No pack is selected or frozen.
